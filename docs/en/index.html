HOW WE KEEP IT TRACEABLE

From hearsay to a source, in three steps

SOURCES / EDIT / LOCALISE

Sources
  1. 01

    Official sources

    Tourism boards, UNESCO and ASEAN materials come first, each logged with a visit date and link; the Philippines, Laos and Myanmar use an open third-party reference, with the tier shown. [1]

  2. 02

    Editorial check

    Dishes and places are checked against the source. Subjective ratings such as heat or English use are labelled “editorial”. [2]

  3. 03

    Localisation

    Chinese, English and Indonesian are rewritten separately; dish names keep the original with a local reading, never literal translation. [3]
